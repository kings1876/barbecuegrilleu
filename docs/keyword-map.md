# Keyword Map — Barbecue Grill Co. (Australia)

Rebuilt 2026-10-12 for Australian searches. Replaces the earlier US-oriented maps.

**Data caveat:** no Search Console, analytics or keyword-tool data was available. Priority and difficulty are
judgement calls from who ranks in Australian results, not measured volumes. Re-score once Search Console
has 4-8 weeks of data.

## Australian wording to use (and avoid)
| Use | Avoid | Why |
|---|---|---|
| BBQ, barbecue, barbeque | "grill" as the only noun | Australians search "bbq" far more than "grill"; "barbecue" is the formal form. Use "BBQ" in titles/H1s, "barbecue" in body copy. |
| LPG, gas bottle (9kg), natural gas | propane, propane tank | Gas BBQs are specified LPG vs natural gas. |
| kettle BBQ, charcoal BBQ | charcoal grill | Weber kettle is the Australian charcoal default. |
| kamado, ceramic BBQ | — | "Kamado" is used as-is. |
| strata, apartment, balcony | HOA | Electric BBQ demand is driven by strata rules. |
| flavour, aluminium, enamelled | flavor, aluminum, enameled | Spelling. |
| A$ / AUD | USD | Already switched. |

## Page-ownership rules
- One cluster per URL. `/` owns the generic "bbq for sale"; `/shop/` is the all-products list and should not compete for the same phrase (differentiate: `/shop/` = "buy bbq online / all bbqs").
- Brand pages own brand + "australia" searches; category pages own type searches; product pages own model names.
- "Barbecue" vs "BBQ": same intent, so no separate pages. Page copy should use both.

## Realistic expectations
- Model-name and brand+type queries (Kamado Joe, Napoleon Rogue, Weber Pulse) are the most winnable: 3-6 months with unique specs and content.
- "gas bbq", "bbq for sale", "charcoal bbq" are dominated by Tucker BBQ, Appliances Online, Bunnings, Harvey Norman, Weber AU: 9-12+ months and needs links/reviews; not a quick win.
- No ranking is promised; all of this depends on competition, authority and time.

## Open issues that affect the map
1. **Pricing:** site prices are the earlier numbers relabelled AUD. Australian retail is well above them (e.g. Genesis E-325 about A$1,529-1,799 vs A$949 on this site). Wrong prices will hurt trust and could mislead buyers. Client needs to supply a real AUD price list.
2. **AU model names/specs:** several listed models are US names (Genesis E-325s, Original Kettle 22"). Verify against Weber/Napoleon Australia pages.
3. **Domain:** `.com` (barbecuegrilleu.com) with no AU geo signal. A `.com.au` needs an ABN and typically an Australian business presence; if bought, redirect and consider hreflang `en-AU`.
4. **Three blog posts** still reference retired placeholder products (Ember 45, Ridgeline Pro, Patio Pro). Their links point to pages that no longer exist, so fix first.
5. **No phone/address/GBP:** local SEO can't start.
